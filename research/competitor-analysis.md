# Competitor research — summary

**Research date:** 25 October 2026
**Method:** web search from this session (US-based index), plus direct URL fetches where a specific
claim needed checking. Structured records in [`competitor-analysis.csv`](competitor-analysis.csv).

**What this is not.** No traffic estimates, no backlink counts, no search volumes, no AI-citation
testing. None of those tools were available, and inventing the numbers would make the file worse
than useless. Every such field in the CSV reads `unverified` or `Not tested`.

## Four findings that changed the recommendations

**1. Nobody publishes a price.** Across the whole set, exactly one provider was reported to publish
course fees. When the URL carrying that figure was fetched directly it returned **HTTP 404**, so no
competitor price is quoted anywhere in this research. The finding that survives is the structural
one: pricing information is almost absent from this market, which is why
`/insights/what-corporate-ai-training-costs/` was written.

**2. The Arabic SERP is the wrong shape for the buyer.** Searching
`أفضل كورس تعلم الذكاء الاصطناعي` returns self-paced technical courses — Python, LangChain,
agentic AI — for individual learners. Searching for corporate executive AI training in Arabic
returns UAE government programmes, a SaaS company's translated listicle, and training institutes.
**No Arabic-language, practitioner-written content about how to buy corporate AI training appeared
in either result set.** That gap is the clearest opportunity in this research.

**3. Nobody shows their work.** Not one competitor examined publishes named client case studies with
documented outcomes. Menova has four. They are currently the most under-used asset on the site,
because nothing outside the site corroborates them.

**4. The peer who is visible is visible for a reason that has nothing to do with his website.**
Zoka's reach comes from community and speaking. The lesson is not to copy his content; it is that in
this category, distribution beats on-page work — which is precisely what the audit concluded from
the other direction.

## Where Menova is genuinely stronger

| Dimension | Position |
|---|---|
| Who delivers | A named individual with a verifiable AUC appointment and a current commercial role. Institutes sell catalogues and deliver through associates |
| Local proof | Four documented Egyptian engagements. No competitor found publishes equivalents |
| Language | Arabic delivery with English materials, by one person, without an interpreter |
| Commercial terms | EGP invoicing by an Egyptian entity; no travel line in Greater Cairo |
| Transparency | After this session, the only page in the set that explains how this category is priced |

## Where Menova is weaker, and it is not close

| Dimension | Position |
|---|---|
| Off-site corroboration | Near zero (audit §8). Every competitor here has at least directory presence |
| Domain authority | A shared `github.io` subdomain against established commercial domains |
| Event and community presence | None. ALX convenes Cairo HR leaders monthly; Zoka has a large community |
| Directory listings | Absent from the listicles buyers use to shortlist |
