## A commercial programme, not an AI programme

Hisense did not need an introduction to generative AI. The team needed to know where it genuinely
helps in a retail go-to-market cycle — and, just as usefully, where it does not.

The programme concentrated on the expensive work: segmentation from real customer data, campaign
concepting, retail execution material, and the go-to-market cadence. Content generation for its own
sake was deliberately not the focus.

## The brand-voice problem

The standard failure mode for AI in a marketing function is copy that is grammatically fine and
completely off-brand — which means it gets rewritten, and the time saving evaporates.

A dedicated block addressed this: using the brand's own style guide as context, and the bilingual
problem specific to this region, where Arabic output frequently reads as translated rather than
written.

## Delivered through Formatech

The engagement ran through Formatech, an established regional training provider. Menova works both
ways — directly with client organisations, and through training partners where a client already has
an arrangement in place.
