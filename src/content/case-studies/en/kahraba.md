## Why this engagement is the reference case

Most corporate AI training starts in a department where someone is already enthusiastic. That
produces a good day and very little organisational change, because the people who allocate budget
and set priorities were not in the room.

Kahraba ran the other way round. The CEO and senior management went first. Once the leadership had
a shared position, the programme extended outward — reaching 65 executives in total.

## The constraint that shaped the design

Kahraba is critical national infrastructure. A significant proportion of the material an executive
works with daily cannot be pasted into a public AI tool under any circumstances.

Rather than treating that as a limitation to work around, the boundary session was moved to the very
front of the programme. Participants agreed what could and could not leave the organisation before
a single exercise ran on real material — which meant the exercises could then use genuinely real
material, rather than the sanitised examples that make most corporate training forgettable.

## What the 4.8 rating reflects

Participants were not rating a lecture. They were rating a programme in which they rebuilt their own
recurring work, on their own documents, and left with a written plan for their own area.
