## The second engagement — and why it matters more

A client that comes back is the real rating. After 65 executives went through the Copilot
programme, Kahraba's CEO and senior management booked a second, harder day: not using AI inside
documents, but delegating whole pieces of work to it — and supervising the result the way an
executive supervises a direct report.

## From assistance to delegation

The organising idea of the day: chat returns an answer you act on; Cowork returns a finished
artefact. Your role shifts from asking questions to writing briefs and holding a review gate. The
failure mode shifts too — from an unhelpful answer to confident work in the wrong direction. The
session drilled the discipline that manages that risk: literal sources, an audit clause in the
standing instructions, "stop and ask" conditions, and Manual permission mode — always, for a
company that moves money and controls physical infrastructure.

## Model routing as a budget decision

The room learned to read the model table the way a CFO reads a vendor deck: Sonnet 5 for routine
volume, Opus 5 as the working default, Fable 5 by exception — for long reasoning chains, large
contradictory document sets, and decisions worth a thousand times the token cost. Vendor-reported
benchmarks were read next to independent ones, deliberately.

## Built for their directorates, not in the abstract

A skills matrix mapped four kinds of skill — Doer, Formatter, Checker, Rule — across seven Kahraba
directorates: Executive, Finance, Projects, Operations, Information Systems, Warehouses and
Tenders. Each participant then built the one for their own directorate live, edited its SKILL.md
before saving — because a skill you did not edit is a skill you do not own — and saw how a plugin
packages it for a whole department.

## The demo the CEO remembers

Two, in fact: a scheduled Monday-morning energy brief that researches the Egyptian electricity
sector, currency movement and fuel pricing before anyone is awake, every claim cited; and a
bid/no-bid analysis of a real tender pack, argued clause by clause. Both are the difference
between talking about agentic AI and watching it do a week's work in minutes — with the
verification discipline to trust the result.
