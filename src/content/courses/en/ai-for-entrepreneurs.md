## A course that ends with a pitch, not a certificate

Most entrepreneurship courses end with a certificate. This one ends with you standing in front of
the class pitching a business project you built across six sessions — with AI doing the heavy
lifting at every step.

## The six-session arc

The programme is sequenced so each session feeds the next. You master the tools first, because
everything after depends on them. You plan the strategy second, because the project needs a
direction before it needs assets. Then you build outward: your professional profile, your
data-driven answers and first no-code apps, and the AI helpers that keep the business running
while you sleep. The final session is the delivery: your project, pitched to the room.

## Built for people building something

This is not a corporate programme adapted for individuals — it is designed for entrepreneurs from
the first hour. The examples are founder problems: explaining a difficult market to yourself
before you bet on it, looking credible online before the first client call, answering questions
with your own data, and automating the routine work no founder has time for.
