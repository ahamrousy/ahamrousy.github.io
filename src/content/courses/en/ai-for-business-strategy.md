## Most organisations have AI activity. Very few have an AI strategy.

There are pilots in three departments, a licence agreement nobody reviews, an enthusiastic manager
in finance, and no answer to the question *what are we actually trying to win?*

**AI for Business Strategy** is sixteen hours built to close that gap — and to close it inside the
room, not in a consultant's follow-up deck.

## Three frameworks, deliberately combined

**SOSTAC** provides the spine: situation, objectives, strategy, tactics, action, control. It stops
the conversation collapsing into a tool discussion.

**Playing to Win** forces the five choices most AI conversations avoid — above all *what will we not
do*. A leadership team that cannot name what it is declining does not yet have a strategy.

**The Strategy Cockpit Canvas** assembles the result onto one page the executive committee can
actually review each month, rather than a sixty-slide deck that is opened once.

## Sixteen hours, honestly used

Day one establishes where the organisation genuinely stands — including an audit of current AI
activity, which in most organisations is the uncomfortable part. Day two makes the choices, sequences
them across four quarters with owners and budgets, and writes kill criteria in advance for every
initiative.

Every choice is defended in front of the other participants before the workshop closes.

## Before anything begins: the boundary

Because the workshop runs on real strategic material, the first session sets the data boundary —
what may be entered into a public AI tool and what may not. It is a short session, and it is not
optional.
