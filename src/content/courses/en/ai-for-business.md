## The problem this programme solves

Most organisations do not have an AI tools problem. They have an AI *judgement* problem. Licences
get bought, a few enthusiasts produce impressive demos, and six months later the weekly report still
takes as long as it did before.

**AI for Business** fixes that by teaching one method the whole team shares. Not a tour of features —
a way of thinking about which work belongs to a machine, how to brief it properly, and how to tell
when the answer is wrong.

## How the two days run

The programme is 70% hands-on. Participants spend most of the session working on tasks from their
own week, on their own documents, with Ahmed reviewing output live. Theory appears only where it
changes a decision — why models hallucinate, why context beats cleverness, why a longer prompt is
usually a worse one.

Every 90 minutes there is a demonstration designed to genuinely surprise the room: a task that
normally takes an afternoon, completed and verified in four minutes, on the client's own data.

## What the team leaves with

A shared prompt library built during the session, three rebuilt workflows per participant, a
verification routine, and a written 30-day adoption plan. Not a certificate and a folder of slides.
