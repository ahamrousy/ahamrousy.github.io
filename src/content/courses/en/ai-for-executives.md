## Executives do not need another tool demo

A senior leader does not need to know how to write a prompt for a social media caption. They need to
know which AI investments are real, which are theatre, what to demand from the people who report to
them, and where the organisation's boundary sits.

**AI for Executives** is one day built entirely around those four questions.

## Built on real decisions, not case studies

Participants bring their own material: a board paper in progress, a proposal from a vendor, a market
question they are genuinely unsure about. The exercises run on that. By the end of the day each
participant has a one-page AI position for their own function — drafted, challenged by peers, and
sequenced across ninety days.

## Where this has been delivered

Ahmed has run executive-level AI programmes for the CEO and senior management of Kahraba, the
National Electricity Technology Company, and for companies affiliated with the Engineering Export
Council of Egypt. Across the Kahraba engagement 65 executives were trained, rating the programme
4.8 out of 5.
