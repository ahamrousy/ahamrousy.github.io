## Taught by someone who has carried the number

Most AI-for-marketing training is delivered by AI specialists who have never owned a marketing
budget. This one is not. Ahmed Amrousy runs marketing and PR for Nile Air, Egypt's largest private
airline, and spent two decades before that leading marketing at Electrolux, Samsung, Cadbury Egypt
and Nahdet Misr.

That shows up in what the programme refuses to teach. There is no time spent on generating social
captions. The two days go to the work that is genuinely expensive: segmentation, brief writing,
bilingual campaign development, competitor reads, and turning sales conversations into assets.

## The Arabic problem, addressed directly

Generative models produce Arabic that reads as translated English. For a regional brand that is not
a small aesthetic issue — it is the difference between copy that lands and copy that gets rewritten.
A dedicated block of the programme deals with this: how to brief for Arabic that reads as written,
and how to hold voice across a bilingual campaign.
