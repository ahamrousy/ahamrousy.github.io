## Arabic delivery, without an interpreter

The single most common complaint about international AI training in the Kingdom is that it arrives
in English, aimed at a Western corporate context, with the local nuance stripped out — or worse,
routed through an interpreter who does not know the material.

Ahmed Amrousy delivers verbally in Arabic. Participants ask questions in Arabic and get answers in
Arabic, from the person who wrote the material. Slides and take-away documents remain in English,
which is what most Gulf corporate environments actually want.

## Built for the Vision 2030 context

Saudi organisations are not asking whether to adopt AI. They are asking which functions to move
first, what to fund this year, and how to avoid a portfolio of pilots that never scales. That is the
question the 16-hour AI for Business Strategy workshop is built to answer — with SOSTAC providing
the structure and Playing to Win forcing the choices, including what the organisation will decline
to do.

## Sectors
