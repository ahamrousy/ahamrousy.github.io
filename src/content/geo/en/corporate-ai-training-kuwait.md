## Arabic delivery, without an interpreter

Most international AI training reaching Kuwait arrives in English, built for a Western corporate
context. Ahmed Amrousy delivers verbally in Arabic, from material he wrote himself, so questions are
asked and answered in the room's own language. Slides and take-away documents stay in English, which
is what most Kuwaiti corporate environments use day to day.

## Your data rules, written down first

Kuwait has no general data protection law. CITRA's Data Privacy Protection Regulation (No. 26 of
2024) governs licensed telecom and IT providers, but for most organisations the question "what can
we paste into an AI tool?" has no external answer. Every Menova programme therefore opens by writing
that answer down: what is safe to use, what must be anonymised, and what never leaves the building.
The rest of the training then runs on realistic, anonymised versions of your own documents.

## Built for the New Kuwait 2035 agenda

Kuwait Vision 2035 aims to move the country toward a knowledge-based economy. For a leadership team,
that turns into practical questions: which functions to move first, what to fund this year, and how
to keep AI pilots from multiplying without ever scaling. The AI for Business Strategy workshop is
built to answer exactly that, using SOSTAC for structure and Playing to Win to force the choices.
