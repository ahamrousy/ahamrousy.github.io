## Already delivered here

Menova is not pitching the UAE as a new market. Ahmed Amrousy has delivered *AI for Marketing,
Retail & GTM Excellence* for Hisense in the UAE, via the training provider Formatech.

## Why UAE commercial teams choose this over a generalist provider

The UAE has no shortage of AI training. What it has less of is AI training delivered by someone who
has actually run a commercial function at scale. Ahmed is Head of Marketing & PR at Nile Air and
spent two decades leading marketing at Electrolux, Samsung, Cadbury Egypt and Nahdet Misr.

For a retail or go-to-market team, that difference shows up immediately: the session goes to
segmentation, campaign development and sales enablement on the client's own data, rather than a tour
of tool features.

## Language flexibility that matters here

UAE rooms are rarely uniform. Some are entirely English-speaking; some are predominantly Arabic;
many are mixed. Menova can deliver either way — verbal Arabic with English slides, or full English —
and the material is identical.
