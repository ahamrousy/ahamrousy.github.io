## Built for the UAE's bilingual workplace

The Emirates runs on mixed teams — Arabic-speaking leadership, multinational staff, English
documentation. Menova's delivery model was built for exactly that: verbal Arabic with English
slides where the room prefers it, full English where it does not, and the same materials either
way. No interpreter between the trainer and the room in either language.

## The programmes UAE teams ask for first

Commercial teams take **AI for Marketing & Sales** — segmentation, bilingual campaign work and
sales enablement on the team's own CRM data. Leadership groups start with **AI for Executives**
and, where a written strategy is needed, continue to the 16-hour **AI for Business Strategy**
workshop built on SOSTAC and Playing to Win.

## Sectors

Retail and consumer electronics, financial services including DIFC and ADGM firms, real estate,
logistics and trade, hospitality, and family conglomerates. Where confidentiality constraints
apply, the data-boundary session comes first — before any exercise touches real material.
