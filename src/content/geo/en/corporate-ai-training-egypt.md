## Trained in Egypt, for Egyptian organisations

Menova is based in Cairo. That is not a detail — it is why the training works. The examples come
from Egyptian companies, the delivery is in Egyptian Arabic, the case material reflects how
decisions actually get made in an Egyptian organisation, and there is no travel cost to add for
programmes in Greater Cairo.

Ahmed Amrousy has spent 25 years inside Egyptian industry — currently at Nile Air, and before
that at Electrolux, Samsung, Cadbury Egypt and Nahdet Misr. He teaches at the American University in
Cairo and is completing a DBA at Ain Shams University.

## Sectors served in Egypt

Energy and utilities, aviation, manufacturing and export, financial services, FMCG, higher
education, and professional services. Where a sector has particular data-sensitivity constraints —
as in energy and financial services — the boundary session is expanded accordingly.

## The most common Egyptian engagement

A leadership team takes AI for Executives first. If the appetite is there, the same group returns
for the 16-hour AI for Business Strategy workshop, and their departments take AI for Business in
parallel. That sequence is what happened at Kahraba, and it is the pattern that produces adoption
that lasts beyond the training week.
