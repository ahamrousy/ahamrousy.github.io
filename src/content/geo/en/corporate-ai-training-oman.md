## Arabic delivery, without an interpreter

Ahmed Amrousy delivers verbally in Arabic, from material he wrote himself. Participants ask in
Arabic and get answers in Arabic, while slides and take-away documents stay in English — the working
mix in most Omani organisations.

## Data protection comes first

Oman's Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree 6/2022) has applied since February 2023, with its
Executive Regulation in force since February 2024. That makes "what can we paste into an AI tool?" a
compliance question, not a preference. Every Menova programme opens by answering it in writing: what
is safe to use, what must be anonymised, and what never leaves the organisation. Exercises then run on
anonymised versions of your own documents, so the training is realistic without the risk.

## Built for Vision 2040

AI is one of the pillars of Oman Vision 2040, and the Ministry of Transport, Communications and
Information Technology runs a national programme for AI and advanced digital technologies. For an
organisation, the practical question is narrower: which work to change first, and how to measure it.
The programmes are built around that question, on your own processes rather than generic demos.
